## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "Cinematic enterprise AI engineering operating system"
- Interpretation: Fast, crisp motion and transitions paired with settled reading holds (minimum 1.2–2.0s per key sentence or metric). Dark terminal aesthetics with high-contrast text and glowing accents.
- Angle: Multi-agent systems fail when they rely on flat unguided prompt chains. NexusSwarm solves this with corporate hierarchical governance, sub-second model routing, and hard AST security gates.
- Hook: A pulsing terminal brings up the question: "What happens when you give 28 autonomous AI agents real engineering tools?"
- Outro / punchline: "Governance enforced at the tool layer."
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS marketing fluff ("supercharge your workflows", "next-gen synergy")
  - Abstract 3D bouncing spheres or meaningless shapes
  - Overly fast cuts that hide the actual metrics
