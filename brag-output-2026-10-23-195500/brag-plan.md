# Brag Plan: NexusSwarm (Slim Edition)

## What is this app?
NexusSwarm is an autonomous multi-agent operating system that organizes 28 specialized AI agents into enterprise engineering divisions (Executive → 6 Managers → 21 Workers) to plan, write, test, secure, and deliver software under runtime tool-layer governance.

## The angle
Most multi-agent frameworks are flat prompt chains that break on real terminals and hallucinate phantom imports. NexusSwarm mirrors corporate execution structures: intelligent NIM routing slashes generation latency by 62%, AST analysis intercepts fake package attacks before execution, and an autonomous QA repair loop patches test tracebacks without human prompts.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Obsidian dark terminal with a luminous emerald hexagon mark `⬡`:
"What happens when you give 28 autonomous AI agents real engineering tools?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Hierarchical Swarm Topology**: Executive Orchestrator coordinates 6 divisions (Planning, Engineering, QA, Security, Reliability, DevOps) with 28 live specialists.
- **Dynamic Model Router (62% Latency Cut)**: Intelligent NIM routing drops latency from 45.2s down to 17.1s.
- **AST Hallucination Defense & Autonomous QA Self-Repair**: Intercepts hallucinated dependencies (`superauth_enterprise_pro` blocked) and auto-patches pytest failures to 100% green tests.

## Outro / punchline
"Governance enforced at the tool layer, not the prompt layer."
Final card lands with the `⬡ NexusSwarm` brand lock and GitHub link `github.com/theshauryas1/nexus-swarm`.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "Cinematic enterprise AI engineering operating system"
- Interpretation: Crisp, high-contrast dark IDE aesthetics; confident, rhythmic pacing with generous holds for reading.

## Duration: 20s
